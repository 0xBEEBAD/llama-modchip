\begin{abstract}
Deploying frontier Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language models---spanning 30B to 284B parameters---on commodity 16~GB, 32~GB, and 64~GB CPU workstations and consumer GPU+CPU hybrids remains bottlenecked by memory capacity, per-token DDR5 bandwidth, and mismatched speculative decoding policies. Standard 2-bit quantizers either exceed host RAM budgets on 176B--284B models or rely on lossy expert pruning and donor transcoding, while naive multi-step neural draft loops waste milliseconds executing full-vocabulary \texttt{lm\_head} projections or over-drafting high-entropy prose on bandwidth-bound MoE backends.

We present \textbf{\texttt{llama-modchip v3.1.0}}, an open-source, hardware-aware inference architecture uniting three co-designed layers: (1) a \textbf{Three-Tier 100\% Donor-Free \texttt{MaxQ} Fleet} spanning 64~GB (\texttt{Qwen3.8-Flash-Next MaxQ-64GB}, 53.99~GiB, blind \texttt{wiki-alt.txt} PPL \textbf{3.4325} and \textbf{$-46.6\%$} KLD vs.\ 54.40~GiB \texttt{IQ1\_M} competitor; plus \texttt{DeepSeek-V4-Flash 284B MaxQ-v4}, 57.99~GiB, 100\% standard \texttt{ggml}), 32~GB (\texttt{Qwen3.8-Flash-Next MaxQ-32GB}, 24.98~GiB resident trunk + 10.73~GiB demand-paged \texttt{PCA-Wiener + RaBitQ Q1\_0} Per-Layer Embeddings verified under strict Cgroup v2 \texttt{28.5 GiB} \texttt{Swap=0}), and 16~GB (\texttt{NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-30B-A3B VQ3-64} at 13.30~GiB and 100\% standard \texttt{ggml} \texttt{MaxQ-16GB} at 13.39~GiB); (2) a \textbf{Zero-Collision \texttt{ggml} ABI} preserving upstream \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q1\_0 = 41}, \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q2\_0 = 42}, and \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_F8\_E4M3 = 43} untouched while assigning custom \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_FRACTAL\_64 = 44} (3.75~bpw) and \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_VQ3\_64 = 45} (3.00~bpw LUT) across AVX-512 VBMI/VNNI, AVX2+FMA, ARM64 NEON, and native Apple Metal GPU kernels with zero-copy 3D MoE \texttt{{-}{-}repack}; and (3) \textbf{\texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} (\texttt{{-}{-}spec-type routecrack2})}, a unified speculative engine fusing NextN Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) / \texttt{RCPredictor}, O(1) Two-Zone 40K Suffix Indexing with sub-250~ns fast-path bypass, and 192~KB Stockfish-PVS Zobrist memory with 28-bit Tagged Mode Refutation under an \textbf{832~B (13-cache-line) L1-resident \texttt{[10$\to$16$\to$1]} SIMD Micro-Arbiter}. On a 16-core Zen~5 CPU (0~GPU), \texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} reaches \textbf{15.88~tok/s aggregate / 21.98~tok/s peak} on \texttt{Qwen3.8} ($+80.8\%$ vs.\ $N=1$, $+14.5\%$ vs.\ \texttt{draft-mtp}), \textbf{7.79~tok/s aggregate / 12.31~tok/s peak} on 284B \texttt{DeepSeek-V4 MaxQ-v4} ($+14.5\%$ vs.\ $N=1$), and \textbf{28.20~tok/s aggregate / 42.18~tok/s peak} on \texttt{Nemotron-30B} ($+24.8\%$ vs.\ $N=1$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures paired with hybrid linear-attention, state-space, or latent-attention compression decouple total model capacity from per-token active FLOPs. Three open-weights flagship families span the modern local deployment spectrum:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{\texttt{Qwen3.8-Flash-Next}} ($176.5\text{B}$ total, $5\text{B}$ active): A 48-layer hybrid architecture combining 36 \texttt{Gated DeltaNet} (GDN) linear-attention layers, 12 Grouped-Query Attention (QSA) layers, 512 routed experts per layer, a massive $320\text{M}$-row Per-Layer Embedding (PLE) table, a $248\text{K}$-token vocabulary, and a built-in \texttt{blk.48} 1-layer NextN Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) module \cite{gloeckle2024mtp}.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731}} \cite{deepseekv3} ($284.33\text{B}$ total, $13.17\text{B}$ active): A 43-layer MoE Transformer combining 64-head Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA, compressed KV latent rank 512, query LoRA rank 1024, 8 output head-groups of rank 1024), sparse \texttt{CSA}/\texttt{LID}/\texttt{HCA} indexer compressors, 4-stream Sinkhorn-20 Hyper-Connections, and 256 routed experts (top-6 active plus 1 shared expert, with deterministic hash routing \texttt{tid2eid} on layers $0..2$).
    \item \textbf{\texttt{NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B}} \cite{nemotron2025} ($30.2\text{B}$ total, $3.0\text{B}$ active): A 52-layer hybrid architecture comprising 23 Mamba-2 State-Space Model (SSM) layers, 23 Sparse MoE layers (128 routed experts with top-6 active + 1 shared expert), and 6 GQA layers.
\end{enumerate}

Prior local inference systems---including PowerInfer \cite{powerinfer}, KTransformers \cite{ktransformers}, SqueezeLLM \cite{squeezellm}, and FlashAttention-2 \cite{dao2023flashattention2}---target either GPU-offloaded dense layers or ReLU sparsity. Running all three modern families cleanly inside 16~GB, 32~GB, and 64~GB commodity workstations on top of \texttt{llama.cpp} \cite{llamacpp} exposes four fundamental engineering walls:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Donor-Free Capacity Wall}: Across 18 published \texttt{Qwen3.8-Flash-Next} GGUF checkpoints (54.40--329.72~GiB), $0/18$ fit inside 32~GB host RAM ($\le 28.5$~GiB) because naive 4-bit PLE quantization consumes 26.82~GiB alone; similarly, uncalibrated 2-bit \texttt{DeepSeek-V4} builds either exceed 64~GiB or degrade severely.
    \item \textbf{ABI Fragmentation Across Quantization Formats}: Repurposing upstream \texttt{ggml} type enum slots causes binary incompatibility whenever upstream introduces standard formats such as \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q1\_0} (41), \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q2\_0} (42), or \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_F8\_E4M3} (43).
    \item \textbf{The Unified Speculative Cost Asymmetry}: On 1-layer NextN MTP models (\texttt{Qwen3.8}), chaining sequential \texttt{draft-mtp} steps incurs $3\times{\sim}15$~ms of $248\text{K}$ \texttt{lm\_head} compute while never invoking suffix memory; conversely, on 284B non-MTP MoE models, drafting unverified prose tokens pulls extra 256-expert weights over DDR5 at $+0.395\times T_1$ latency per token.
    \item \textbf{3D MoE Repack Memory Blowup \& Thread-Pool Convoys}: Default heap repacking on 3D MoE tensors (\texttt{ne[2] >= 64}) destroys zero-copy \texttt{mmap} sharing (inflating \texttt{RssAnon} $5.4\times$ to 9.81~GiB), while oversubscribed \texttt{-t N {-}{-}poll 50} threads induce severe OS barrier convoys.
\end{itemize}

\section{Three-Tier Donor-Free \texttt{MaxQ} Compression \& Zero-Collision ABI}

\subsection{Zero-Collision \texttt{ggml} Type ABI (\texttt{41..45})}
To guarantee simultaneous compatibility with upstream \texttt{llama.cpp} formats and custom block-64 CPU/Metal kernels inside one binary, \texttt{llama-modchip v3.1.0} leaves all upstream enums completely untouched and places custom kernels at dedicated non-colliding IDs (Table~\ref{tab:abi}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Upstream Standard Types Preserved}: \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q1\_0 = 41} (RaBitQ 1-bit PLE), \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_Q2\_0 = 42} (18~B / 64~W $= 2.25$~bpw signed-scale), and \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_F8\_E4M3 = 43}.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{Fractal-64} (\texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_FRACTAL\_64 = 44}, 30~B / 64~W $= 3.75$~bpw)}: Stores an FP16 super-scale $d$, eight 4-bit shifted sub-scales $\tilde{m}_s = m_s + 4 \in \{4..19\}$, and 64 3-bit indices into the kurtosis-matched dyadic codebook $\{-14,-8,-4,-1,+1,+4,+8,+14\}$. Achieves \textbf{17.75~dB SQNR} (99.15\% cosine similarity on raw \texttt{BF16} MoE weights, $+1.45$~dB over \texttt{Q3\_K}). Sub-scale multiplication fuses into \texttt{vpmaddwd} at zero extra instructions, \texttt{qh} unpacking leverages AVX-512 \texttt{vprorvq}, and native register-tiled Apple Metal shaders sustain \textbf{78.60~tok/s} on M3 Max and \textbf{57.65~tok/s} on M1 Max.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{LUT-VQ3-64} (\texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_VQ3\_64 = 45}, 24~B / 64~W $= 3.00$~bpw)}: Encodes 60 weights as twenty 7-bit indices into a 128-entry 3D spherical Lloyd-Max codebook (384~B held across six AVX-512 \texttt{ZMM} registers) plus 4 tail weights and eight 3-bit sub-scales $\tilde{m}_s \in \{2..9\}$. Decoded in-register via three AVX-512 VBMI \texttt{VPERMI2B} (\texttt{\_mm512\_mask2\_permutex2var\_epi8}) lookups (with portable AVX2, ARM64 NEON, and scalar fallbacks), achieving \textbf{13.57~dB SQNR} ($+3.42$~dB over \texttt{Q2\_K}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Mathematical Co-Design: \texttt{Woodbury DualGram}, Low-Rank SVD, \& $L_2$-Quadrature MoE Pruning}
\label{sec:math}

\subsection{FP64 Woodbury DualGram \& \texttt{ConeCollapse}}
When quantizing a weight matrix $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}} \times d_{\text{in}}}$ against $N_C \ll d_{\text{in}}$ routed calibration activations $\mathbf{H}_c \in \mathbb{R}^{N_C \times d_{\text{in}}}$ (e.g., $N_C = 384$, $d_{\text{in}} = 2048$), forming the full $d_{\text{in}}\times d_{\text{in}}$ Hessian $\mathbf{H}_c^\top \mathbf{H}_c + \lambda \mathbf{I}$ is both ill-conditioned and computationally prohibitive across 256--512 experts per layer. Invoking the Woodbury matrix identity reduces Hessian inversion to an $N_C \times N_C$ FP64 Cholesky dual solve:
\begin{equation}
    (\mathbf{H}_c^\top \mathbf{H}_c + \lambda \mathbf{I})^{-1} = \lambda^{-1}\left(\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{H}_c^\top (\mathbf{H}_c \mathbf{H}_c^\top + \lambda \mathbf{I}_{N_C})^{-1} \mathbf{H}_c\right),
\end{equation}
maintaining condition number $\kappa_2 \le 86.33$ while capturing cross-channel activation covariance. Under \texttt{ConeCollapse}, pruning low-energy intermediate channels ($K_{\text{full}} \to K_{\text{red}}$) is paired with a post-quantization shrunk residual centroid bias $\mathbf{b}_{\text{down}} = \gamma\,\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{W}_{\text{down}}\mathbf{h} - \widehat{\mathbf{W}}_{\text{down}}\hat{\mathbf{h}}]$ (\texttt{GGML\_OP\_ADD\_ID}), preserving downstream residual mean and variance across all 34--42 collapsed layers.

\subsection{Spectral Decay of $\mathbf{W}_{o,b}\mathbf{W}_{o,a}$ \& $L_2$-Quadrature MoE Tail Pruning}
In \texttt{DeepSeek-V4-Flash}, 31.0\% of per-token DRAM traffic (991.6~MB/tok) traverses the unactivated MLA output chain $\mathbf{y}_{\text{attn}} = \sum_{g=1}^{8} \mathbf{W}_{o,b}^{(g)}(\mathbf{W}_{o,a}^{(g)} \mathbf{x}_g)$. Exact SVD reveals rapid spectral decay ($\sigma_0 = 15.398 \to \sigma_{768} = 0.901 \to \sigma_{1023} = 0.048$): rank-768 truncation ($-25\%$ DRAM) preserves 99.64\% of Frobenius energy and 0.998204 output cosine similarity.

For optional runtime MoE tail pruning on learned-router layers ($l \ge 3$, leaving hash-routed lexical layers $l \in \{0,1,2\}$ at full Top-6), let $\mathbf{y}_{\text{FFN}} = \mathbf{e}_{\text{shared}} + \sum_{i=1}^{K} w_i \mathbf{e}_{k_i}$ with sorted weights $w_1 \ge \dots \ge w_K > 0$:

\begin{proposition}[$L_2$-Quadrature vs.\ Linear $L_1$ Rescaling]
Let routed expert outputs $\mathbf{e}_{k_i} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ ($d=4096$) be zero-mean, equal-variance ($\mathbb{E}\|\mathbf{e}_{k_i}\|_2^2 = \sigma_e^2$), and nearly mutually orthogonal ($\mathbb{E}\langle \mathbf{e}_{k_i}, \mathbf{e}_{k_j}\rangle \approx 0$ for $i \neq j$), orthogonal to $\mathbf{e}_{\text{shared}}$. When truncating to $1 \le k < K$ experts with multiplier $\alpha$, preserving the exact expected squared norm entering downstream \texttt{RMSNorm} requires \textbf{$L_2$-quadrature rescaling}:
\begin{equation}
    \alpha_{L_2} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{K} w_i^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{k} w_i^2}},
\end{equation}
whereas linear $L_1$ rescaling $\alpha_{L_1} = (\sum_{i=1}^K w_i)/(\sum_{i=1}^k w_i)$ strictly inflates routed-branch variance against $\mathbf{e}_{\text{shared}}$ by $\Gamma(w) = \alpha_{L_1}^2 / \alpha_{L_2}^2 > 1$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By orthogonality, $\mathbb{E}\|\sum_{i=1}^k (\alpha w_i)\mathbf{e}_{k_i}\|_2^2 = \sigma_e^2 \alpha^2 \sum_{i=1}^k w_i^2 = \sigma_e^2 \sum_{i=1}^K w_i^2 \implies \alpha = \alpha_{L_2}$. For typical $l \ge 3$ weights at $k=4$, $\alpha_{L_1}=1.2295$ ($\Gamma=1.388$), whereas $\alpha_{L_2}=1.0435$.
\end{proof}

Empirically on \texttt{DeepSeek-V4 284B}, switching from $\alpha_{L_1}$ to $\alpha_{L_2}$ at $k=4$ cuts top-10 next-token KL-divergence by \textbf{58.1\%} ($0.0671 \to 0.0281$~nats), while $k=5$ ($\alpha_{L_2}$) incurs only 0.0042~nats ($+0.42\%$ local $\Delta\text{PPL}$). Unpruned Top-6 (DeepSeek/Nemotron) and Top-10 (Qwen3.8) remain strictly default in \texttt{v3.1.0}.

\section{\texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} \& the 832~B L1 Micro-Arbiter}
\label{sec:spec}

\subsection{The CPU-MoE Speculative Horizon Law}
Unlike GPU speculative decoding \cite{leviathan2023fast} where batched verification costs nearly the same as a single forward step, on memory-bandwidth-bound CPUs hosting Sparse MoE models verifying $k$ drafted tokens in a batched pass incurs non-zero marginal cost $C(k) > C(0) = 1.0$ because distinct draft tokens activate additional routed experts from DDR5 ($\Delta C \approx 0.285\,T_1$ on \texttt{Qwen3.8}, $0.310\,T_1$ on \texttt{Nemotron-30B}, and $0.395\,T_1$ on 284B \texttt{DeepSeek-V4}), plus an optional neural draft pass cost $C_{\text{mtp}} \approx 0.13\,T_1$ and rollback penalty $C_{\text{rb}}$. Given conditional per-step acceptance probabilities $q_0, q_1, \dots, q_{k-1}$, the expected accepted yield of a $k$-token draft is:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbb{E}[A_k] = 1 + \sum_{d=0}^{k-1} \prod_{j=0}^{d} q_j, \qquad R(k) = \frac{\mathbb{E}[A_k]}{C(k) + C_{\text{draft}}}.
\end{equation}
Appending token $t_d$ is profitable \emph{if and only if} $R(d+1) > R(d)$ under a conservatism guard $\kappa \ge 1.03$.

\subsection{Unified Three-Phase Speculative Pipeline}
Implemented in \texttt{common/rc\_routecrack2.h} and \texttt{common/rc\_arbiter\_l1.h} (with 100\% byte-identical copies tracked under \texttt{speculative/cpp/}), \texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} (\texttt{{-}{-}spec-type routecrack2}) unifies three orthogonal candidate generators:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Phase A --- Sub-250~ns Fast-Path Two-Zone Suffix Bypass}: Maintains an incremental O(1) 3-gram hash-chain index spanning a \textbf{16,384-token Hot Zone} and a \textbf{24,576-token Prefix Zone} with O(1) rollback undo ($<100$~ns lookup at 64K context). When context consensus satisfies the arbiter's \texttt{fastpath\_q0\_min} gate (0.895 on MTP models) and is unrefuted in Stockfish TT, \texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} drafts up to 5 tokens in $<250$~ns, completely bypassing the ${\sim}15$~ms 248K MTP \texttt{lm\_head} pass.
    \item \textbf{Phase B --- Neural MTP / \texttt{RCPredictor} Anchor + Anchored Extension}: When native MTP is present (\texttt{n\_layer\_nextn > 0}, \texttt{Qwen3.8}), runs a \emph{single} $d=0$ NextN forward pass extracting top-4 probabilities and logit margin, arbitrates \texttt{mtp\_t0} against Stockfish \texttt{tt6..tt3}, and anchors \texttt{[prompt..., id\_last, mtp\_t0]} back into the Two-Zone Suffix index and Stockfish TT to extend up to 5 tokens while keeping \texttt{ctx\_dft} 100\% KV-position-aligned across partial acceptances. On non-MTP models (\texttt{Nemotron-30B} \& \texttt{DeepSeek-V4 284B}), fuses \texttt{RCPredictor} (8-level FNV-64 Hash Cascade + 300 hot rules + 11,382 macro chains + 248K-safe online \texttt{RCTrie}/PPM/Skipgram, requiring \texttt{trie\_obs >= 2} on prompt-warmed candidates to prevent single-hit self-corroboration) with Two-Zone Suffix.
    \item \textbf{Stockfish-PVS 192~KB Zobrist TT \& 28-Bit Tagged Mode Refutation}: Drawing on Principal Variation Search transposition tables and history heuristics \cite{stockfish}, tracks verified \texttt{best\_move}, explicit \texttt{refuted\_move}, and history gravity $\in [-512,+512]$ across three lockless 4-way 64~KB tables (\texttt{tt6..tt3}) plus collision-free \textbf{28-bit Tagged Mode Refutation} isolating JSON, Code, and Prose session heat so syntax-mode transitions never trigger stale refutations (eliminating \texttt{W2} JSON divergence on both \texttt{DeepSeek-V4} and \texttt{Nemotron}).
    \item \textbf{Phase C --- 832~B L1-Resident SIMD Micro-Arbiter (\texttt{rc\_arbiter\_l1.h})}: Evaluates a 10-feature vector (\texttt{mtp\_p1}, \texttt{mtp\_margin}, \texttt{suffix\_match\_len}, \texttt{suffix\_hit\_count}, \texttt{zone\_is\_hot}, \texttt{sf\_tt\_wins}, \texttt{sf\_tt\_history}, \texttt{depth\_d}, \texttt{recent\_accept\_ema}, \texttt{is\_structural\_tok}) through a cache-line-aligned \texttt{[10$\to$16$\to$1]} ClippedReLU MLP with pre-sanitized \texttt{NaN}/\texttt{$\pm$Inf} inputs and a degree-5 Remez minimax \texttt{fast\_sigmoid} ($<3\times 10^{-6}$ max error). Each weight profile occupies \textbf{832~B (13 cache lines, 100\% L1d-resident)} and executes via unaligned \textbf{AVX-512F} (\texttt{\_mm512\_loadu\_ps}), \textbf{AVX2+FMA} (\texttt{\_mm256\_loadu\_ps}), \textbf{ARM64 NEON} (\texttt{vfmaq\_n\_f32}), or portable C++17 scalar code with 100\% cross-ISA bit-identity on adversarial \texttt{NaN}/\texttt{$\pm$Inf} inputs. At model load, the engine automatically selects \texttt{kRC2DefaultArbiter} (\texttt{n\_layer\_nextn > 0}), \texttt{kRC2DraftlessArbiter} (non-MTP $\le 35$~GiB), or \texttt{kRC2HeavyMoEDraftlessArbiter} (non-MTP MoE $>35$~GiB, plus $n_{\max}=3$ cap on dense CPU models to fit one \texttt{mr=4} GEMM pass).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Microarchitectural \& Multi-Platform Systems Engineering}
\label{sec:sys}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{512-Bit \texttt{Q1\_0}/\texttt{Q2\_0}/\texttt{MXFP4} Kernels \& Multi-Threaded \texttt{GATED\_DELTA\_NET}}: Dedicated AVX-512 VNNI/BW single- and multi-column (\texttt{1x8/4/3/2}) kernels accelerate \texttt{Q1\_0} (41), signed-scale \texttt{Q2\_0} (42), overflow-free \texttt{Q8\_0}, and \texttt{MXFP4}, while fused 8-\texttt{zmm} FMA multi-threaded \texttt{GATED\_DELTA\_NET} removes inner barriers across all 36 \texttt{Qwen3.8} linear-attention layers and restores dynamic $4\cdot n_{\text{th}}$ causal-triangle load balancing on \texttt{FLASH\_ATTN\_EXT} prefill.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Copy 3D MoE \texttt{{-}{-}repack} \& \texttt{q8\_cache} Aliasing Cure}: Fixes upstream \texttt{ggml\_compute\_forward\_mul\_mat\_tiled} / \texttt{mul\_mat\_id\_tiled} \texttt{q8\_cache} workspace aliasing, clears \texttt{last\_mmid\_ids\_data} on non-\texttt{MUL\_MAT\_ID} nodes, and skips heap repacking on 3D MoE tensors (\texttt{ne[2] >= 64}) so zero-copy \texttt{mmap} is preserved (\texttt{Qwen3.8 MaxQ-64GB} \texttt{RssAnon} drops $5.4\times$ from 9.81~GiB to \textbf{1.81~GiB}, \texttt{batch=1} \texttt{{-}{-}repack} vs.\ \texttt{{-}{-}no-repack} $\text{KLD}=0.00000$).
    \item \textbf{Non-Convoy Threading, Inward 2~MiB THP, \& Hardened Per-UID IPC}: Pairing \texttt{-t (N\_CORES - 1) -tb (N\_CORES - 1) {-}{-}poll 0 {-}{-}poll-batch 0} eliminates OS barrier convoys ($+25.9\%$ \texttt{pp512}, $+31.3\%$ \texttt{tg128} over oversubscribed \texttt{-t N {-}{-}poll 50}). \texttt{MADV\_HUGEPAGE} in \texttt{src/llama-mmap.cpp} aligns inward to 2~MiB boundaries under \texttt{THP=madvise} so lazy PLE pages are never pre-faulted. All runtime control and HARP-Cache shared-memory files are isolated per-UID (\texttt{0700} dir / \texttt{0600} mode, \texttt{O\_NOFOLLOW}, explicit \texttt{DSV4\_MOE\_CFG\_PATH}), with zero world-writable global temp paths.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{/dev/shm} Hybrid State Snapshots}: Saving and restoring a 512-token \texttt{Qwen3.8} hybrid state (233.14~MiB $= 36$ GDN \texttt{[48$\times$128$\times$128]} FP32 + 12 QSA \texttt{Q8\_0} KV + 1 checkpoint) completes in \textbf{38.49~ms} average (6.11~GiB/s, $162\times$ faster than cold prefill; \textbf{19~ms} with \texttt{{-}{-}ctx-checkpoints 0}) with 100\% bit-exact greedy continuation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations \& Engineering Trade-offs}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Custom Format Portability vs.\ Universal \texttt{MaxQ}}: While \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_FRACTAL\_64 = 44} ships native CPU and Apple Metal GPU shaders and \texttt{GGML\_TYPE\_VQ3\_64 = 45} runs on AVX-512 VBMI/AVX2/NEON/scalar, neither has CUDA/Vulkan GPU shaders. Deployments targeting CUDA, Vulkan, or unmodified upstream \texttt{llama.cpp} should select the 100\% standard-\texttt{ggml} editions (\texttt{Qwen3.8 MaxQ-64GB/32GB}, \texttt{DeepSeek-V4 MaxQ-v4}, or \texttt{Nemotron MaxQ-16GB}).
    \item \textbf{High-Entropy Prose on 284B Non-MTP MoE}: On \texttt{DeepSeek-V4 MaxQ-v4}, \texttt{W4} free prose experiences a $-7.4\%$ slowdown vs.\ $N=1$ (6.49 vs.\ 7.01~tok/s) because 256-expert DDR5 pull cost dominates whenever context n-grams miss; aggregate throughput still improves by $+14.5\%$ due to $+90.0\%$ \texttt{W1} and $+12.7\%$ \texttt{W2} gains.
    \item \textbf{Low-Bit Sampling Hygiene on \texttt{MaxQ-32GB}}: At 1.63~bpw, greedy $T=0$ sampling can trigger tail repetitions on long generations; applying \texttt{{-}{-}temp 0.6 {-}{-}top-p 0.95 {-}{-}top-k 20 {-}{-}presence-penalty 0.2 {-}{-}repeat-penalty 1.05} (automated by \texttt{./modchip}) restores 100\% loop-free generation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\texttt{llama-modchip v3.1.0} demonstrates that pairing donor-free \texttt{MaxQ} compression (\texttt{PCA-Wiener + RaBitQ Q1\_0} PLE, FP64 Woodbury DualGram, and \texttt{ConeCollapse}), a zero-collision multi-ISA \texttt{ggml} ABI (\texttt{44}/\texttt{45}), zero-copy 3D MoE \texttt{{-}{-}repack}, and the \texttt{RouteCrack 2.0} 832~B L1 Micro-Arbiter brings frontier 30B--284B models onto commodity 16~GB, 32~GB, and 64~GB workstations with verified speed and quality parity across Linux, macOS Metal, and Windows. Full source code, unified patches, preflight verification tools, and raw benchmark artifacts are published at \url{https://github.com/synchopate/llama-modchip}.
